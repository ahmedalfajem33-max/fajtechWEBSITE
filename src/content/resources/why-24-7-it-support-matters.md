Many small businesses assume 24/7 IT support is something only large enterprises need. But the problems that cost you the most rarely wait until Monday morning.

## Attacks happen when nobody is watching

Ransomware groups deliberately launch attacks at night, on weekends and over holidays — when they know offices are empty and nobody will notice encryption spreading across the network until it is too late. Round-the-clock monitoring means someone can isolate an infected computer at 2 am instead of discovering the damage at 8 am.

## Your business may not keep office hours either

- Restaurants, retailers and hospitality businesses are busiest in the evenings and on weekends.
- Construction, energy and logistics operations run early starts and long shifts.
- Healthcare clinics open evenings and Saturdays.
- Staff travel, work from home and answer email late at night.

When your point-of-sale, scheduling system or email goes down outside 9-to-5, you need help *then* — not the next business day.

## Downtime is expensive

Every hour your team cannot work costs wages, lost sales and customer goodwill. Fast, round-the-clock response turns what could be a lost day into a short interruption.

## What real 24/7 support looks like

Not every provider that says "24/7" means the same thing. Ask:

1. **Does a real technician answer, or just a voicemail?**
2. **Is security monitoring continuous, with someone able to respond — not just alerts that wait in an inbox?**
3. **Is after-hours support included, or billed extra?**
4. **How do I reach you at night — phone, WhatsApp, a portal?**
