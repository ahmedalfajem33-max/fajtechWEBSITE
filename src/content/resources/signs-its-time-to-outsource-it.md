Many growing businesses run their IT the same way: an office manager, a tech-savvy employee or the owner fixes things when they break. That works — until it doesn't. Here are seven signs you have outgrown it.

## 1. The same problems keep coming back

Slow computers, Wi-Fi drop-outs, printers that stop working every week. Recurring issues mean nobody is fixing the root cause — only the symptoms.

## 2. Your "IT person" has a different full-time job

When your best salesperson or office manager spends hours fixing laptops, you are paying a skilled employee to do work outside their role — and their real job suffers.

## 3. You don't know if your backups work

If nobody can tell you when your backups were last tested — or whether Microsoft 365 is backed up at all — you are one ransomware attack or hardware failure away from losing everything.

## 4. Security is an afterthought

No multi-factor authentication, no staff training, no one watching for threats at night. Small businesses are now the most common target for cybercriminals precisely because they are less protected.

## 5. Problems happen after hours with nobody to call

Servers fail and attacks happen at night and on weekends. If help is only available 9 to 5 — or not at all — every incident becomes a crisis.

## 6. Technology is slowing down growth

Onboarding a new hire takes days. Opening a new location feels overwhelming. Your software doesn't talk to each other. The right IT should make growth easier, not harder.

## 7. You can't predict your IT costs

Surprise invoices, emergency hardware purchases and unbudgeted projects make planning impossible. Managed IT turns that into one predictable monthly fee.
